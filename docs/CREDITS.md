# Screenshot photos

The photos in the README screenshots are public-domain images from NASA
([images.nasa.gov](https://images.nasa.gov/)): art002e009280b (Artemis Era
Earthrise), iss072e159172 (aurora borealis from the ISS), GSFC_20171208_Archive_e001788
(Blue Marble 2012), GSFC_20171208_Archive_e000842 (Pillars of Creation, Hubble),
KSC-20250115-PH-FMX02_0001 (launch streak), iss023e058455 (Earth observation).
NASA does not endorse pixvault.

`fixtures/crew.jpg`, used by the face-detection test, is NASA's Expedition 61
crew portrait (jsc2019e022584, public domain), reduced to 640 px.
